# Ship 30 for 30 Content Writing Skill

## Philosophy & Core Principles

The Ship 30 for 30 methodology transforms dense domain knowledge into clear, skimmable, and actionable thought-leadership essays. When writing about product and growth topics using Lenny's Podcast transcripts:

### 1. The Irresistible Hook (0-10% of essay)
- Start with a compelling headline and a 1-2 sentence opening hook.
- Directly frame the high-stakes problem operators face (e.g., onboarding drop-off, PMF stagnation).
- Avoid fluff, throat-clearing, or generic rhetorical filler.

### 2. High-Skimmability & Rhythm (10-80% of essay)
- Use strong, descriptive H2 and H3 subheadings every 2-3 paragraphs.
- Short sentences and short paragraphs (max 2-3 sentences per paragraph).
- Use bulleted and numbered lists for frameworks, steps, and key questions.
- Apply **selective bolding** on counter-intuitive insights and key principles so a reader scanning in 30 seconds absorbs the core ideas.

### 3. Clear Narrative Progression
- Follow the 4-part arc:
  1. The Common Mistake / Hidden Friction
  2. The Shift in Perspective
  3. The Concrete Framework (grounded in operator tactics from Lenny's guests)
  4. The Step-by-Step Playbook

### 4. Grounded Claims & Exact Attribution
- Every tactical insight must cite the relevant guest and context (e.g., *"As Adam Fishman proved at Patreon..."*, *"Elena Verna emphasizes..."*).
- Never invent quotes, metrics, or frameworks not present in the transcripts.

### 5. The Specific, Useful Takeaway (Last 10%)
- Conclude with a punchy, actionable takeaway or checklist that the reader can implement on Monday morning.

### Target Length
- Target word count: **approximately 1,250 words** (acceptable range: 1,000 to 1,400 words).
